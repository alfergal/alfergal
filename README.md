## About

I'm a **Data Scientist and Senior Software Developer** with **5+ years of professional software-development experience**, combining production engineering with machine learning, predictive analytics and generative AI.

My background spans the full software lifecycle: from building data pipelines and predictive models with **Python, Pandas, SQL and scikit-learn**, to designing scalable frontend architectures with **Angular and TypeScript**, developing Drupal/PHP solutions, integrating APIs, automating tests and working with CI/CD environments.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **software engineering and data**: turning ideas and models into maintainable products that can actually be shipped.
